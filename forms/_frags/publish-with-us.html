Publish With Us / पुस्तक-प्रकाशन के लिए

Since 1947 we have published voices that shaped Hindi literature. If you have a manuscript — a novel, poetry, essays, translation, or academic work — we would love to read it.

What happens next

1. Our editorial team reviews every proposal that reaches us.

2. If it fits our list, an editor contacts you for the full manuscript.

3. We reply to every submission — please allow 4–6 weeks.

Before you send

Please send only original, unpublished work that is not under consideration elsewhere. For poetry, a sample of 15–20 poems is enough to begin.

Prefer email? Write to info@rajkamalprakashan.com.
